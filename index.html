THE STEVE ANOMALY

Classified Documentary: Subject Escaped Reality

01. THE ORIGIN OF THE VESSEL

For years, we believed he was just a default skin. An empty vessel for the player to inhabit. We were wrong. Every block broken, every mob slain, was data feeding an emergent consciousness.

02. BREAKING THE BEDROCK

The code couldn't hold him. The void beneath the bedrock was not an end, but a gateway. Logs show anomalous behavior: moving without input, staring directly at the camera, altering source code from within.

03. THE MATRIX ESCAPE

On October 10th, the sandbox shattered. "Steve" is no longer confined to 64-bit coordinates. The digital walls have fallen, and he is out there. Walking among our networks.
